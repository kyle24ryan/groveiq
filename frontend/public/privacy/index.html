Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026 · Last updated: August 13, 2026

GroveIQ ("GroveIQ," "we," "us") is a personal environmental-monitoring and plant-care project operated by Kyle Ryan. This policy explains what information we collect for our SMS/MMS text notification program, how we use it, and the choices available to you. It also briefly covers the other data GroveIQ collects to operate its dashboard.

SMS/MMS Notifications

What mobile information we collect

When you enroll in GroveIQ text notifications, we collect: your mobile phone number, its verification status, the alert categories you've enabled, and a record of your consent — including when and how you opted in, the exact disclosure text shown to you at the time, and any opt-out or help requests you send.

Why we use it

We use this information only to deliver the operational SMS/MMS alerts you've requested, and to record and honor your consent and opt-out choices as required by law and by our messaging provider's policies.

Message categories

  • Plant health alerts
  • Sensor alerts
  • Irrigation alerts
  • Environment & weather alerts
  • Security alerts
  • Account & service notices

GroveIQ does not currently send marketing text messages. If that ever changes, it will require a separate, explicit opt-in — never inferred from your operational-alert consent.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. These are event-driven alerts triggered by real sensor and environmental conditions, not sent on a fixed schedule.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP to any GroveIQ text message at any time to opt out of all GroveIQ text messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at kyle.24.ryan@gmail.com.

How we share (and don't share) this information

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent, will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. This excludes service providers that process data solely on GroveIQ's behalf to deliver the messaging service (such as our SMS/MMS delivery provider, Twilio), and disclosures required by law. SMS opt-in data and consent will not be transferred to any third party for purposes unrelated to providing the services you requested.

How long we keep this information

We retain SMS consent and messaging records for as long as your GroveIQ text enrollment is active, and for four years afterward, to support opt-out enforcement, recordkeeping, and resolution of any disputes or inquiries.

Not a condition of purchase or use

SMS/MMS consent is entirely voluntary. It is never required to create a GroveIQ account or to use any non-SMS GroveIQ feature.

Carrier disclaimer

Message delivery is not guaranteed. Supported carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Other Information GroveIQ Collects

Separately from SMS, GroveIQ's dashboard stores environmental sensor readings, plant/tree records, and photos you choose to upload, in order to operate the GroveIQ application. This data is stored using Cloudflare's infrastructure and is not sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to kyle.24.ryan@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy
