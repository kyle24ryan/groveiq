Program description
GroveIQ sends optional, opt-in text message alerts related to your GroveIQ-monitored plants and environment, including plant health, sensor, irrigation, environmental/weather, security, and account/service notifications that you individually enable.
How to enroll
Enroll from Notification Settings by entering your mobile number, reviewing the disclosure shown, and affirmatively checking the consent box. You'll receive a one-time verification code by text; after verifying your number, you can enable the specific alert categories you want to receive.
Consent is voluntary
Consent to receive SMS/MMS messages is not a condition of purchase, account creation, or use of any other GroveIQ feature.
Message frequency
Message frequency varies, based on real-world conditions and the categories you've enabled.

Opting out

Re-enrollment
You may reply START to re-subscribe. New affirmative consent, given through Notification Settings, is required to re-enable specific alert categories after opting out.
